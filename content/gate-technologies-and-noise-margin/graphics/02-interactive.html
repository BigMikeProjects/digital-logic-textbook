Definitions
D or Esc closes
Supply voltage, VDD
The voltage that powers the gate; the top of the picture. A 1 lives near VDD, a 0 near 0 V.
VOH,min
The output's promise for a 1: it will be at least this high.
VOL,max
The output's promise for a 0: it will be at most this high.
VIH,min
What an input needs to read a 1: at least this high.
VIL,max
What an input needs to read a 0: at most this high.
Forbidden zone
Between VIL,max and VIH,min. The input promises nothing there: it may read a 0, a 1, or neither.
Noise margin, NM
The gap between what the output promises and what the input needs: NMH = VOH,min − VIH,min, NML = VIL,max − VOL,max. It is the noise a wire can pick up before a bit is misread.
JEDEC
The electronics industry's standards body (originally the Joint Electron Device Engineering Council). It publishes the voltage levels for each supply (3.3, 2.5, 1.8, 1.2 V and more) so chips from different makers can talk to each other.
TTL, CMOS
Two ways to build gates. TTL (transistor-transistor logic) uses bipolar transistors; CMOS uses the pull-up and pull-down MOSFETs of Transistors to Gates. CMOS won because it draws almost no power while idle.
