DefinitionsEsc or D to close
Tclock period — the time from one rising clock edge to the next. tcoclock-to-output delay — from the clock edge until Q shows the new value.FPGA datasheets quote clock-to-output at the chip’s pins. That figure is larger: it includes the clock wiring and the output buffer. tpdlogic propagation delay — how long the logic between two flip-flops takes to settle after its inputs change. tsusetup time — how long D must be steady before the clock edge. thhold time — how long D must stay steady after the clock edge. decision windowfrom tsu before the edge to th after it; D must not change inside it. tskewclock skew — when the edge reaches the capture flip-flop minus when it reaches the launch flip-flop. Positive: capture clock late. Negative: launch clock late. slackthe time left over; negative slack means a violation.
