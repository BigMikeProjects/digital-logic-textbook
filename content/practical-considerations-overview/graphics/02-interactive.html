DefinitionsEsc or D to close
T
clock period — the time from one rising clock edge to the next.
tco
clock-to-output delay — from the clock edge until Q shows the new value.
FPGA datasheets quote clock-to-output at the chip's pins. That figure is larger: it includes the clock wiring and the output buffer.
tpd
logic propagation delay — how long the logic between two flip-flops takes to settle after its inputs change.
tsu
setup time — how long D must be steady before the clock edge.
th
hold time — how long D must stay steady after the clock edge.
decision window
from tsu before the edge to th after it; D must not change inside it.
slack
the time left over in the clock period; negative slack means a violation.
metastable state
Q hovering between 0 and 1 before it settles to one of them.
